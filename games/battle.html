Courage Under Fire: Battle

In development Far future · Army-scale · Companion to Skirmish

You have fought the skirmish — the knife-fight in the ruins. This is the hammer. A battle line two metres long. Tanks grinding through rubble. Fire falling from a sky you cannot see. An army is only squads that have not broken yet.

Free rules — coming soon PDF Support the project

Battle takes the engine you already know and scales it to war. The core loop does not change — action, reaction, exposed, the three states that decide everything. What changes is everything around it. Five models become fifty. One activation becomes a tide of them.

Massed fire does not kill. It pins, it batters, it strips the cover away. The killing is done by the weapons that were always going to do it — the plasma, the fusion, the laser cannon, the blade in the leader's hand. Find the gap that volume fire opens. Put the heavy weapon through it.

You do not win by killing everything. You win by breaking their nerve and holding the ground when they run. Because an army does not die to the last man. It breaks.

What scales

01

The world, not the bookkeeping

Scale the war, not the spreadsheet. A Skirmish player sits down and already knows how to play — five models become fifty without new arithmetic.

02

Squads as texture

The squad is texture, but the soldier who matters is still a soldier. Massed fire pins hardened troops; it doesn't quietly delete them from a health bar.

03

Armour & heavy weapons

Tanks grinding through rubble and the killing weapons that punch through the gap volume fire opens.

04

Nerve and the break

You win by breaking their nerve and holding the ground when they run. Morale is the real objective.
