A Wounds & Steel game · Core system

Courage Under Fire

In development World War II · Infantry · 2d6 engine

A fiction-first tactical TTRPG of small-unit infantry combat. You are not a hero. You are infantry. The bullet doesn't care about your level; the mortar does not distinguish veteran from conscript. What keeps you alive is cover, communication, and the soldier next to you.

Free rules — coming soon PDF Support the project

In the mud outside Stalingrad, in the bocage of Normandy, in the tunnel darkness of the Pacific — combat is grounded, lethal, and personal. Built on a 2d6 engine where every roll carries consequence, the game puts three decisions at the heart of every firefight: your action, your reaction, and whether you are exposed.

Every weapon behaves differently. The bolt rifle rewards patience. The LMG controls space. The shotgun clears rooms. No role is safe and no level makes you invulnerable — your squad survives together, or it doesn't survive at all.

The war does not end when the session ends. The wounds stay. The decisions haunt.

What's inside

01

Five battlefield roles

Leader, Medic, Gunner and more — two specialisation paths each, built for genuine squad interdependence. No role is safe.

02

Armoured combat

A full system for vehicles: penetration, targeted effects, and crew stress. Armour is cover until it isn't.

03

20 campaign modules

Historical missions across six theatres of war, from the Eastern Front to the Pacific, with 16 ready-to-run enemy units.

04

Universal terrain

Winter, night operations, jungle, desert, tunnels, mud and rain — the ground fights you as hard as the enemy does.

05

Suppression & stress

Pinned and stressed soldiers don't act freely. Morale and fear are mechanics, not flavour text.

06

Wounds that stay

A wound track, aftermath rolls, and memorable marks. Recovery is never guaranteed, and the cost is remembered.
