A Wounds & Steel game · Skirmish

Courage Under Fire: Skirmish

In development Far future · Elite vs horde · 41st Millennium edition

Two players. One elite kill-team. One larger force. Neither side fights the same way. The elite operatives are hard to kill and individually lethal; the larger force is fragile but numerous, coordinated, and capable of pressure the elite side cannot ignore. Cover is your armour. Suppression is your weapon.

Free rules — coming soon PDF Support the project

The larger side activates more models per round. Each activation pressures the elite side — draining their reactions, forcing them into cover, stripping their defences. When an elite operative is exposed and out of reactions, the larger side commits their heaviest weapons. That moment must be manufactured. That is what the game is about.

The elite side's game: eliminate the enemy's ability to coordinate. Kill the leader. Kill the special weapons. Hold objectives. Never let them create the moment.

You do not need to eliminate the enemy. Holding ground wins the game. Killing everything is a strategy, not a requirement.
