Why this exists

Good games should be reachable. The rules that make a battle worth playing don't belong behind a paywall — they belong on the table, dog-eared and dice-scarred.

Rules are free because the experience is yours.

This is a solo passion project. Every system here is grounded in the real texture of combat — distance, cover, the cost of a wound — whether the soldiers are men in 1944 or something far stranger. If the work earns a place on your table, a donation keeps the next manual coming.

How it plays

M-01

Measured ground

Every move and shot is paid in inches. Range and cover decide more than any dice roll.

M-02

Wounds, not hit points

Soldiers don't have health bars. They bleed, they break, and a single wound can end a plan.

M-03

One engine, many worlds

Learn the core once and carry it from history to fantasy to the far future without relearning.

M-04

Table in minutes

Free PDFs, light bookkeeping, proxies welcome. Print the rules tonight, play tomorrow.
